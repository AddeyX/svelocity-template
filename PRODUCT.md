# Product

## Users

One person, with a short private list of notes. The public site is for someone who wants to hear when the product exists. There is no team, no shared workspace, and no second account inside a note.

## Product purpose

The demo turns the template into something you can run. Sign in, write a note, delete it through a confirmation, and join a waitlist from the public page. That is the whole product.

Success is a checkout that already has a styled sign-in screen, a list, and a public page on the same tokens.

## What it is not

Not a task manager, a CRM, or a multi-user notebook. Those stay in the products this template was drawn from.
